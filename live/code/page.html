Hoop Impact Bench

Hoop Impact Bench tests NBA player impact metrics (EPM, DARKO, LEBRON, BPM and others) on games played after their ratings were saved. In each game, every player's rating is weighted by the minutes he played, which gives each team a rating in points per 100 possessions. The home team's rating minus the away team's is the predicted margin, and the boards measure how closely the predicted margins follow the final margins.

2026-27 is the current NBA season, which opened on October 20, 2026. The retro tables cover the six seasons before it.
