Hoop Impact Bench

An open-source benchmark for NBA player impact metrics. Each metric's ratings are saved before the games, then tested on how well they predict the final margins. Code, data and results are public and can be rerun: github.com/lumahoops/hoop-impact-bench.

Hoop Impact Bench tests NBA player impact metrics (EPM, DARKO, LEBRON, BPM and others) on games played after their ratings were saved. In each game, every player's rating is weighted by the minutes he played, which gives each team a rating in points per 100 possessions. The home team's rating minus the away team's is the predicted margin, and the tables measure how closely the predicted margins follow the final margins.

2026-27 is the current NBA season, which opened on October 20, 2026. The retro tables cover the six seasons before it.

The preseason board uses the ratings each metric had published before opening night of the 2026-27 NBA season (October 20, 2026). These ratings stay fixed, and every 2026-27 regular-season game is computed using them.

Columns

  • Net error: the average miss, in points per 100 possessions, between each team's rating and its opponent-adjusted margin, after each metric's team ratings are shifted so its average team is 0 (the adjusted margins average 0). Lower is better. This is the ranking test: it checks whether the players' ratings add up to the teams' results. The small numbers are its 90% range.
  • Raw: the same miss at face value, before the shift. Hover a raw number for the metric's offset, its average team rating: raw² = net error² + offset². The shift is one number per metric, computed from the ratings and minutes only, never from the results.
  • Game R²: the share of the variation in final game margins that the predicted margins explain. Higher is better.
  • Win % r: the correlation between team ratings and team winning percentage. Higher is better.
  • W-L: each game counts as a win when the team the metric rates higher won (no home-court edge, the same for every metric).
  • Rank: the range of places on net error a metric reaches across 2,000 resamples of the games (the middle 90%). Metrics whose ranges overlap are not separated by the games so far.
  • Italic rows: reference points, not ranked. RAPM fit on these same games (an upper reference), the average of all metrics, last season's RAPM, and two floors.

Views

  • Full forecast (the default): Every player counts at the metric's own rating, rookies and low-minute players included. A player the metric does not rate counts as -2.0.
  • Established players: A player with fewer than 250 minutes the season before, including every rookie, counts as -2.0 for every metric (Taylor Snarr's 2020 rule), and so does a player the metric does not rate.

Labels

  • archived: published by the maker before opening night, with a public date (archive snapshot, release or commit)
  • saved: copied before opening night, with no public record of the date
  • backfill: taken later from the maker's view of a past date, which shows its current model

Sources for the tests: game margins and team net error follow Taylor Snarr's 2020 metric comparison; team win % r is Krishna Narsu's 2024 test; the 250-minute cutoff is Neil Paine's. Questions or errors: open an issue.

Retro 2020-21 to 2025-26

2020-21 to 2025-26, computed using the ratings each metric had before each season.

7,230 games · 180 team-seasons

? What this table shows

Hoop Impact Bench tests NBA player impact metrics (EPM, DARKO, LEBRON, BPM and others) on games played after their ratings were saved. 2026-27 is the current NBA season, which opened on October 20, 2026. The retro tables cover the six seasons before it.

The retro tables compute each season from 2020-21 to 2025-26 (7,230 games, 180 team-seasons) using the ratings each metric had before that season (HIB v2.0.0).

The retro tables are a loose baseline for comparing metrics, not a conclusive ranking. HIB did not receive any of the past ratings before each season was played, so it cannot confirm that a metric's fitting held out the seasons it is scored on; how fully each one did varies (README, the metrics table). The 2026-27 boards are out of sample for every metric by construction: each rating is saved, and its fingerprint published, before the games it is scored on.
