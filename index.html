How to read it:

  • Game R²: how much of each game's final margin the ratings explain, weighted by who played. Higher is better.
  • Win % r: how closely team ratings follow team win %. Higher is better.
  • Net error: how far team ratings miss each team's point margin per 100 possessions. Lower is better, shown once every team has played 20 games.
  • Rank: the range of places a metric takes in 90% of 2,000 resamples. Overlapping ranges mean the games so far cannot separate those metrics.
  • Players with under 250 minutes last season, rookies included, count as -2.0 for every metric.
  • Italic rows are reference points, not ranked.

Labels:

  • archived: the maker published these ratings before the games, and the date can be checked
  • saved: we saved these ratings before the games; no public record shows the date
  • backfill: we took these ratings later from the site's view of that date, which runs its current model

Data: frozen.json, every game, history. Scored with eval.py v1.1.0 (sha256 cfde3f922e88...).

Retro: 2020-21 to 2025-26

We scored each past season with the ratings each metric had before it: 7,230 games and 180 team-seasons, 2020-21 to 2025-26 (HIB v1.1.0).

These ratings come from each site's current model, not what it published at the time. A model can fit past seasons it was built on, so the 2026-27 boards are the stricter test.
